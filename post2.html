Wild beauty

My little self knew it already. While she as sitting on the table, looking at her big blue book, she had the feeling that one day she will go there.
The illustration of animals, the pictures of the nature amazed her.
They were telling her "Be patient, you will grow up and then you will come to us, you will see us"
And then I grew up, and the email arrived.
I was asked to participate in fieldwork in South Africa. I think I screamed a bit. Then i called my family. And the next moment I was already taking vaccines. It was in Jan of 15th. A sunny and warm day.
